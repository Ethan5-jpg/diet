# Same-visit absolute glucose: fixed meal-support completion

This extension was specified after inspection of the previous candidate results and is exploratory. It retains the four selected taxa, both original overlapping common samples and the fixed joint thresholds of at least 5 meals/3 dates, 10 meals/5 dates and 20 meals/7 dates. Higher thresholds filter participants, retaining all of each selected participant's original eligible meals and frozen summaries. No microbiome date-order exclusion, new fixed-14-day completeness gate, exposure restandardization or additional candidate selection is introduced.

The original completed gut-meal run supplies participant-level means and meal membership. The previous absolute-glucose run supplies 24 common-adjustment estimates and 72 original meal-model estimates, together with the six groups' participant membership and hashes. Both runs' file contents, source linkage, counts, common members, adjustment sets, original references and BH calculations are verified before reuse. The original 48-model numerical reproduction is a verified record from the previous run; these 48 models are not refitted in this extension. Meal counts match original membership. Date counts are inherited from the hash-verified participant table, since the membership table does not contain individual meal dates.

Pre-meal glucose is the original baseline summary from each eligible meal's 30-minute pre-meal window. Net increment is the signed net area over 0–120 minutes divided by 120 minutes. Absolute post-meal mean equals baseline plus this net increment on the same meals, then averaged within participants. It represents the time-averaged post-meal level, not a single 120-minute measurement. Negative net areas are retained. Original local CGM and intake-spacing quality criteria remain in force.

For each taxon, pre-meal glucose, net increment and absolute post-meal mean use the original pre-meal model covariates: age, sex, education, smoking, usual sleep, usual physical activity, vitamin and hormone use, BMI, device, corrected hPDI, NOVA4, daily energy, alcohol, meal carbohydrate, mean squared meal carbohydrate, meal energy, hour sine/cosine, previous-intake gap, observation day and duration. This common adjustment excludes pre-meal glucose and slope. The four taxa are fitted separately. Participants receive equal regression weights; original CLR-Z scales are preserved. The original OLS implementation, categorical encoding, HC3 standard errors and residual-df t inference are reused.

The completed common-adjustment table has 72 rows: 24 baseline-threshold rows reused from the preceding common panel; 16 pre-meal rows reused from the preceding higher-threshold original models; and 32 newly fitted net-increment/absolute-mean specifications at the two higher thresholds. The reused higher-threshold pre-meal coefficients have exactly the common adjustment set. Their estimates, standard errors, confidence intervals and p values are retained, but q values are recomputed in the common panel's 12-test family.

Within each sample and threshold, all four candidates by all three common outcomes form one fixed BH family (q_panel12). Missing p values remain in the planned denominator using p=1 internally, while displayed q values stay missing. Fewer than 50 participants, insufficient variation, unstable/rank-deficient designs, fewer than 30 residual degrees of freedom or extreme leverage yield NOT_ESTIMATED with a reason. Every planned row is retained and no threshold is relaxed.

The prior original-model table is preserved independently: its net-increment and peak-rise models additionally adjust for pre-meal level and slope. Original q_family values refer to four Net or eight Pre/Peak tests per sample/threshold; q_meal12 is also retained. New common-adjustment Net results do not replace the original primary analysis. The combined 144-row display includes the same 24 pre-meal estimates in both blocks; these repeated references are not independent findings.

Twenty-four checks of beta(Abs)=beta(Pre)+beta(Net) under identical X are algebraic validation only. Each confidence interval is obtained directly from its own fit; intervals are not added. These identities do not quantify mediation or explained proportions. BH families do not claim global control over all samples, thresholds and both model blocks.

Six-group participant retention and retained/excluded characteristics are reported. Standardized mean differences use the square root of the average group variances. Zero-variance separation, identical constants and inadequate group sizes are explicitly labelled. The compact summary excludes meal/date counts when choosing its maximum characteristic difference. Changes across support groups can reflect both participant composition and measurement support. Crossing a significance threshold is not a formal between-group effect-difference test.

Forest plots show all four candidates and three thresholds for both overlapping samples, with participant counts, pointwise HC3 95% intervals and explicit NE labels. Within each outcome, x-axis ranges are shared across samples. Colors/shapes encode support thresholds, not significance. Complete full-precision effect, p and q values accompany the plots. Synthetic validation figures are labelled separately. Actual source code/dependency hashes, input/output hashes and software versions are stored in the run manifest.

All conclusions remain selected-candidate, same-cohort conditional associations. Strict and higher-support groups are overlapping subsets, not independent validation. Treating measures as belonging to the same visit does not establish causal ordering. Sources and prior results are read-only; identifiers remain in server-private audit tables.
