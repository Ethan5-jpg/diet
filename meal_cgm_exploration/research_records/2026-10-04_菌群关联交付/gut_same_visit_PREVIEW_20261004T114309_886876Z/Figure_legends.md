Figure 1. Same-visit associations of four previously selected microbial candidates with meal glucose phenotypes. Panels show mean postprandial increment (A; original primary outcome), pre-meal glucose (B) and peak rise (C). Circles and squares show the primary and overlapping strict samples. Points are adjusted coefficients per original CLR-Z unit; error bars are pointwise 95% confidence intervals. Family q values are shown at right; the original families comprise four net-increment tests and eight pre-meal/peak tests per sample. Supplementary q12 values for the twelve meal tests are retained in the full server tables; unshown preview values remain missing. See Methods for covariates and meal eligibility. No additional 14-day missingness or microbiome date-order filter is applied.

Figure 2 (full server export only). Same-participant, same-connection whole-CGM reference associations for mean, CV and SDw. Units are original CGM Z per original CLR-Z; q uses twelve reference tests per sample. These models use background/diet covariates and may share readings with meal analyses.
