# Three-species fiber surface across disjoint dates

This discovery experiment transfers the colleague's v9 three-species dose surface to the archived matched calendar-day cohort. Exposure is mean fiber density (g/1000 kcal). Outcome is mean glucose (mg/dL). People with at least four matched dates contribute their earlier half and later half, with any extra day assigned to the later half. The primary comparison uses early diet and late glucose. Reverse dates assess directional consistency within the same participants. All-date means provide a concurrent sensitivity analysis. The visit-level v9 and the split-date experiments have different outcomes and analysis populations.

All comparisons reuse the archived participant and household assignments and split-date main effects. The common adjustment also contains oral and gut ecology and its squares, five PCs per site, and the main linear and median-hinge terms of both microbial markers. Fold-specific origin, scaling, knots, residualization and microbiome PCA use only training participants. The standardized fixed score comes from the archived v3 function for the sum of relative abundances of R. bromii, B. adolescentis and E. rectale. The Shannon-only comparator uses the linear dose interaction on this common adjustment.

The fixed transfer uses four dose × score products. The fiber basis has a straight term and a hinge at 12.5 g/1000 kcal, with curvature residualized on the straight term. The score basis has a straight term and an above-training-median hinge. Ridge penalties per training participant are 0.01 for the straight dose products and 0.03 for curvature. This is a transfer specification with common split-date adjustment and scaling.

Two further alternatives use the three-species panel alone or jointly with gut Shannon. Three-way household cross-validation entirely inside each training fold chooses separate penalties for straight and curved dose products. The grid is (0.003, 0.01, 0.1, 1) × (0.003, 0.03, 0.3, 3). No interaction is also eligible. Each inner split refits its own microbiome preprocessing. Outer performance uses two archived repeats of five household folds. The two repeats share participants.

Report the baseline and interaction R², absolute paired gain, and 100 × gain / baseline R² when positive. All routes share one baseline per direction and repeat. Compare the nested and fixed routes using paired differences and conditional 95% household bootstrap intervals from 1,999 draws. These are exploratory comparisons with no multiplicity adjustment. The separate, unpenalized three-species association is the difference between the upper and lower score quartiles in the 10 to 15 g/1000 kcal fiber contrast, with household-clustered inference. No reserved validation outcomes enter this search.
